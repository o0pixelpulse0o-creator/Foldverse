About FoldVerse

FoldVerse is a student business that transforms ordinary paper into amazing 3D creations. We create handmade crafts, decorative models, pop-up cards, desk accessories and educational projects.

Our goal is to combine creativity, design, innovation and business skills while creating products that are affordable, eco-friendly and visually impressive.
